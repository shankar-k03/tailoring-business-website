# ✂️ Tailoring Studio — Modern Business Website

A modern, responsive and interactive website designed for a tailoring business to establish an online presence, showcase products, accept home-measurement requests, and make customer communication easier.

The project focuses on combining **premium visual design, smooth animations, business functionality, and a mobile-first experience** using simple web technologies.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Traditional tailoring businesses often depend mainly on walk-in customers and phone calls.

This project explores how a local tailoring shop can move part of its business online through:

- 🏠 Professional business website
- 📏 Home measurement booking
- 👔 Dress & product showcase
- ⭐ Customer reviews
- 📱 WhatsApp booking integration
- 📦 Order tracking interface
- 📞 Easy customer contact
- 📱 Fully responsive design

The website is designed as a foundation that can later be connected to a real database and business management system.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏠 Home Page
- Premium hero section
- Business introduction
- Call-to-action buttons
- Animated visual elements
- Smooth scrolling navigation

### 📏 Home Measurement Booking

Customers can request a tailor visit directly from the website.

The booking form collects:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Address
- Area
- Preferred date
- Preferred time
- Tailoring requirements
- Additional notes

The submitted information can be sent directly through **WhatsApp**.

---

### 👕 Product Showcase

A dedicated product section allows the business to display:

- Product image
- Product name
- Price
- Description
- Product details
- Available styles

Products open inside an interactive modal for a better browsing experience.

---

### ⭐ Customer Reviews

A dedicated review section helps build trust by displaying customer feedback and ratings.
